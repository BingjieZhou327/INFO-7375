## 8. Rebuilding from scratch versus checking an existing cut
A fresh source rebuild starts with `make_sheet.py` and `assemble_scenes.py`. An incremental rebuild starts from the **local timestamped `beat_sheet.json` with existing audio and stamps** and calls `./art run`. A quality recheck of a finished export can be done without rerendering using `final_frame_check.py --mp4 <file>`.

**Important:** The show-tell skill's stock bookends require the toolkit host's branding, while the course explicitly forbids the instructor's persona and credit in this student's film. The editing choice and any house-style gate warnings must be recorded honestly instead of claiming they were never present.
